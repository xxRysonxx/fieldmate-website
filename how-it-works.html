Questions

Things people usually ask.

Do I need to change my business phone number?

No. FieldMate connects to the number you're already using on your trucks, website, and business cards. Customers never see or need a new number.

Do I have to install an app or log into a dashboard?

No app or dashboard is required for day-to-day use. Job notifications come straight to your phone, and you can reply from your own messages app.

What happens if FieldMate can't answer something?

Anything outside a normal conversation, like pricing negotiations or complaints, gets flagged and sent straight to you with the full conversation attached, so you can jump in.

Does it work with the calendar or software I already use?

FieldMate can connect with your existing scheduling workflow so appointments fit into the way your business already operates.

How long does setup actually take?

Connecting your number takes about 10 minutes. After that, we configure your business details, test the system, and complete onboarding with you before launch.

Do I have to manage FieldMate after setup?

No. Once your system is configured, FieldMate runs in the background and handles missed opportunities automatically. We manage the setup so you can focus on your customers and your work.

Will customers know they are talking to an automated system?

FieldMate handles routine conversations using your business information and communication style. When a customer needs a person, your team is alerted with the conversation details so you can step in.
