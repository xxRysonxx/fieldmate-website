Questions

Automated review requests, answered.

What are automated Google review requests?

Automated Google review requests are messages sent to customers after a completed job, making it easy for them to leave feedback on Google.

How do automated review requests help get more Google reviews?

By asking at the right time and making the process simple, more customers are likely to leave a review without your team needing to follow up manually.

Can review requests be sent by text message?

Yes. FieldMate sends SMS review requests with a direct link, allowing customers to leave a review quickly from their phone.

Why are Google reviews important for service businesses?

Google reviews build trust, improve local search visibility, and help potential customers choose your business over competitors.

What is the best time to send a review request?

The best time is shortly after a job is completed, when the customer experience is still fresh.
