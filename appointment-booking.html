What is automated appointment booking?

Automated appointment booking helps customers schedule service appointments without back-and-forth communication. It allows businesses to move from inquiry to confirmed appointment faster.

How does appointment booking automation work for contractors?

FieldMate collects customer details, helps guide the conversation, and moves customers toward scheduling an appointment. It reduces delays and simplifies the booking process.

Can appointment booking reduce missed appointments?

Yes. Automated reminders help keep customers informed and reduce no-shows, making your schedule more reliable.

Is appointment booking software useful for service businesses?

Yes. Any service business that relies on fast scheduling can benefit from automated appointment booking. It helps secure jobs faster and reduces the chances of customers contacting another company.

Does automated appointment booking replace my current scheduling process?

No. It works alongside your existing workflow, helping you book appointments faster while keeping your schedule organized.
