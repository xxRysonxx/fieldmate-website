Questions

What is a lead follow-up system for service businesses?

A lead follow-up system automatically responds to new leads and continues follow-up until the customer replies or books a job.

Why is fast lead follow-up important?

Customers usually choose the first business that responds. Fast follow-up helps you connect sooner and win more jobs.

How does automated lead follow-up work?

FieldMate sends an instant response when a lead comes in and continues follow-up messages automatically until the conversation progresses.

Can a lead follow-up system recover lost leads?

Yes. Automated follow-up helps re-engage leads that would otherwise be forgotten or ignored.

Does automated follow-up replace my team?

No. It supports your team by handling responses and follow-ups so they can focus on booking jobs and serving customers.
