Frequently Asked

Questions, Answered.

Everything you need to know before getting FieldMate working for your business.

What exactly is FieldMate? +

FieldMate is a managed revenue recovery system for home service businesses. It follows up with missed callers and leads, helps recover cold opportunities, requests Google reviews, reconnects with past customers, and runs seasonal campaigns.

Do I need to learn new software? +

No. FieldMate is designed to keep things simple. Your team handles the work as usual, and when a job is completed, you update the customer. FieldMate handles the follow-up from there.

What happens when I miss a call? +

FieldMate automatically sends the caller a text message and starts the conversation, giving you a chance to recover the opportunity instead of letting the caller disappear.

What happens if a lead goes cold? +

FieldMate follows up with the lead automatically. If they still don't respond, the opportunity can be flagged for your attention so it doesn't quietly disappear.

Can FieldMate handle customer reviews? +

Yes. When a job is completed, FieldMate can automatically send a Google review request to the customer and notify you if they respond.

Can I bring past customers back? +

Yes. FieldMate can reconnect with past customers and run campaigns designed to create new opportunities from people who have already done business with you.

What does setup include? +

We configure FieldMate around your business, including your follow-up system, customer communication, review requests, reactivation, campaigns, and handover processes.

How much does FieldMate cost? +

FieldMate is $97 per month with a one-time setup fee of $297. Everything is included in the single plan.
